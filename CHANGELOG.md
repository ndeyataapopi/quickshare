## [v0.3.0] — 2026-08

### Added

- Private beta launch with modular Laravel architecture.
- KYC submission, encryption, scanning and review.
- Trust score and tier-based loan limits.
- Loan application, approval, marketplace and funding.
- Manual funding, disbursement and repayment workflows.
- Affordability assessment (advisory).
- Activity and audit logging.
- Collections and fraud monitoring.
- Admin panel with role-based access control.

### Changed

- Referral-only registration during private beta.
